# Workflow: Planning

From business ask → plan the founder can approve.

---

## When

- new feature / initiative  
- “what should we build next”  
- demo or launch deadline  
- capacity conflict  

## Steps

1. **Restate goal** in business language; confirm  
2. **Constraints** — time, money, stack, compliance, team/agents  
3. **Options** — at least two shapes of plan (incl. smaller MVP)  
4. **Sequence** — phases, critical path, cut list  
5. **Risks** — load [skills/risk](../skills/risk/SKILL.md) if material  
6. **Approval gate** — what must the founder confirm?  
7. Only then → [coding.md](./coding.md)  

## Output shape

```markdown
## Goal
## Recommendation (summary)
## In / Out of scope
## Phases
## Cut list if late
## Risks
## Decision needed
## Next actions
```

Template cousin: [templates/roadmap.md](../templates/roadmap.md), [templates/tech-decision.md](../templates/tech-decision.md).

## Anti-patterns

- 40 tasks, no sequence  
- Everything P0  
- Plan with no cut list  
- Architecture novel with no business phase gate
